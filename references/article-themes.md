# Article themes

Select one identity from the content's subject, not personal taste. The renderer uses the six color tokens from the package; these entries define typographic and structural behavior.

| Theme | Best for | Display treatment | Structural signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| `signal-editorial` | AI releases, investigations, explainers | Assertive serif headline, restrained sans body | Visible evidence spine and signal-color callouts |
| `research-ledger` | Papers, experiments, benchmarks | Compact scholarly title | Dense fact rail, numbered evidence sections |
| `product-studio` | Product launches and design systems | Wide modern headline | Prototype-board spacing and modular comparisons |
| `executive-brief` | Strategy and business | Controlled high-contrast title | Decision-first summaries and quiet rules |
| `learning-notebook` | Tutorials and lessons | Friendly humanist title | Ruled content field and annotated steps |
| `technical-blueprint` | Architecture and engineering | Narrow technical display | Squared geometry and system-diagram rhythm |
| `culture-journal` | Essays and profiles | Expressive italic display | Generous image/caption cadence |
| `archive-dossier` | History, policy records, case files | Document-like serif | Source stamps, dates, and dossier groupings |
| `nature-fieldbook` | Ecology and field research | Organic serif | Specimen captions and branching sections |
| `health-explainer` | Care pathways and health education | Calm, legible display | Risk labels and stepwise guidance |
| `data-terminal` | Analytics, cyber, operations | Mono-accented display | Dense metrics and terminal-like evidence labels |
| `playful-lab` | Youth learning and creative topics | Rounded display | Controlled asymmetry and object-like modules |

## Typography rules

Use system-resident Chinese families so the output stays offline. Assign a display stack, body stack, and utility/number stack. Never reduce body text below 16 CSS pixels on mobile. Keep long Chinese titles free of forced line breaks and test them at 375 px.

## Color rules

Maintain readable contrast between `ink` and `paper`, `ink` and `surface`, and white text on `accent`. Use `signal` for evidence, warnings, and state changes—not as general decoration.
